How to get your dental practice recommended by ChatGPT

More patients now ask AI assistants like ChatGPT to recommend a dentist, and the answer usually names only a few practices. There is no switch to flip. These are the signals we review in every dental report and work on in Done For You.

Google Business Profile

Accurate categories, services, hours, and photos, with replies to patient reviews. It is one of the places search tools check first for local businesses.

Consistent listings

The same practice name, address, and phone number on your website, Google, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, Healthgrades, and other dental directories.

Clear treatment pages

A page for each main treatment and location that plainly says who it is for, which insurance you take, and how to book.

Structured data

Dentist and FAQ schema markup, so search engines and AI assistants can read the facts on your site without guessing.

Crawl access

A site setup and robots.txt file that do not block the search crawlers ChatGPT search relies on, including OpenAI's own search crawler.

Genuine reviews

Recent reviews from real patients on the sites AI answers cite. The fix list shows where you are thin. We never write or buy reviews.

How does ChatGPT decide which dentists to recommend?

When a patient asks ChatGPT for a dentist with web search on, it searches the web, reads what it finds, and writes a short answer that names a few practices. OpenAI says ChatGPT search works with third party search providers and can use a person's approximate location for local results. It can only name practices it can find and understand, so clear and consistent information across your website, your Google Business Profile, other listings, review sites, and dental directories matters. No one outside OpenAI controls the answer, and answers can change from day to day.

How long do changes take to show up in ChatGPT?

There is no fixed timeline, and anyone who promises one is guessing. Website copy and structured data can only be picked up after search engines recrawl your pages, which often takes days to weeks. Listings and reviews build up over months. That is why every monthly plan includes a rescan, so you can see what changed instead of taking our word for it.
