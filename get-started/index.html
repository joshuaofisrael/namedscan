Questions

What is AI visibility?

AI visibility is whether AI assistants like ChatGPT name your business when a local customer asks for a recommendation, such as the best plumber or dentist in your city. ChatGPT with web search reads your website, listings and reviews, then names only a few businesses. If it cannot find and understand you, it names someone else.

Can you guarantee I will be recommended by ChatGPT?

No, and nobody honestly can. No one outside OpenAI controls what ChatGPT says, and answers change from day to day. We work on the signals it relies on and measure the results on your plan schedule so you can see what is changing.

Do I have to get on a sales call?

No. You can choose a plan and pay online in a few minutes. If you have questions, email us and we reply in writing.

What happens right after I pay?

Stripe sends your receipt. At checkout you tell us your website, city and business type, and we use that to run your first AI visibility check. We then email you to confirm your details and start the work for your plan.

Is there a contract?

No. Every monthly plan can be cancelled anytime from the link in your Stripe receipt or by emailing us.

How is this different from regular SEO?

SEO is about ranking in search results. AI visibility is about whether ChatGPT names you in its answer. They overlap, since ChatGPT draws on the same listings, reviews and websites, so our work helps with both.

Which plan should I pick?

Most local businesses pick Done For You at $300 a month, because we make the changes for you. Pick Starter at $199 if you prefer to make changes yourself with our guidance, or Growth at $500 if you have several locations or want competitor tracking and a monthly strategy call.
