Privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026

NamedScan is operated by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our AI visibility scans, and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you request a scan or contact us, you send us your name, email address, business name, website, city or service area, business category, and anything else you choose to include. The form on this site opens your own email app, so your request reaches us as a normal email.

Information we collect when running a scan

To build your report we use publicly available information about your business (such as your website and public listings) and the answers returned by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini to customer style questions. The questions we send describe your business category and location. We do not send your personal contact details to these services.

How we use information

  • To run your scan and email you the report.
  • To reply to your questions and provide any service you sign up for.
  • To send occasional updates about your scan or plan. You can ask us to stop at any time.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs. See the GitHub privacy statement for details.

Service providers

We use email and document tools to deliver reports, and third party AI services to collect answers during scans. If we add payments later, a payment processor will handle card details; we will not store full card numbers.

How long we keep information

We keep scan requests and reports for as long as needed to provide the service and track changes over time, and delete them on request unless we must keep them for legal or accounting reasons.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing joshuaofisrael@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Children

NamedScan is a business service and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above.
