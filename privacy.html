Privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026, updated 8 October 2026

This policy explains what information Joshua Israel Ventures LLC ("we", "us") collects through namedscan.com and the NamedScan service, and how we use it. NamedScan is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC.

Who is responsible for your information

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller, meaning the business responsible for your personal information. Contact: joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

The free preview form

When you run a free preview, the form sends your name (optional), email address, business name, website, city, state, and business type to our scanning service (namedscan-api.joshofisrael.workers.dev), which runs on Cloudflare Workers and stores records in a Cloudflare database. If you arrived from a campaign link, your browser also sends the campaign details from that link (utm values, gclid, or fbclid). Our scanning service does not store those campaign details. We keep your free preview request, together with a summary of its result, so Joshua can follow up about your scan. To enforce one free preview per person, we keep a one way hash of your email address and of your IP address, not the IP address itself.

Subscriber scans

If you have a paid plan and enter your email and access key on the scan form, we check them against our records. Your browser saves your email and access key in its local storage (namedscan_sub) so you do not need to retype them.

Information we collect when running a scan

To build your preview or report, we read your public website (homepage, robots.txt, llms.txt, and sitemap) and send customer style questions that describe your business type and location to OpenAI's API, which answers with live web search. We do not send your personal contact details to OpenAI, and we send these requests with OpenAI's storage setting turned off. Scan results are cached for 24 hours so repeat scans of the same business are fast. Paid reports are stored with your email so you can open them from your private link.

Stripe checkout

Payments are handled by Stripe on Stripe's checkout pages. Stripe collects your card and billing details under its own privacy policy. We never see or store full card numbers. From Stripe we receive your email address, the business name, website, city and state, and business type you enter at checkout, the plan you bought, the amount paid, Stripe's customer, subscription, checkout and invoice ids, and the partner code from your visit, if there is one. We use this to create your access key, run and deliver your report, manage your subscription, and pay partner commissions.

Emails

We email your report link, the report PDF, and your access key (for monthly plans) to the email address you gave at checkout, and we reply to emails you send us. These emails are sent from joshuaofisrael@gmail.com using Google's Gmail. If you contact us or apply to the partner program, we keep the emails you send.

Cookies and browser storage

  • ns_ref (cookie and local storage, 90 days): set only when you arrive through a partner's link. It holds only the partner code, which we pass to Stripe checkout so the partner can be credited if you buy.
  • ns_attr (cookie and local storage, 90 days): set only when you arrive from a campaign link. It holds the campaign details from that link (utm values, gclid, or fbclid). When there is no partner code and the link was a paid campaign, we pass a short campaign id to Stripe checkout.
  • namedscan_sub (local storage): set only for subscribers, as described above.

We do not currently use any analytics or advertising tags or cookies. If we ever turn any on, we will update this policy first.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs. See the GitHub privacy statement. Cloudflare, which runs our scanning service, processes your IP address when your browser connects to it.

Service providers

We use GitHub to host the website, Cloudflare to run the scanning service and store scan records, OpenAI to collect ChatGPT answers, Stripe to process payments, and Google (Gmail) to send and receive email. They process information on our behalf to provide these services.

How we use information

  • To run your scan and show or email you the results.
  • To reply to your questions and provide any service you buy.
  • To send occasional updates about your scan or plan. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To credit and pay referral partners.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How long we keep information

We keep scan requests and reports for as long as needed to provide the service and track changes over time, and delete them on request unless we must keep them for legal or accounting reasons.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing joshuaofisrael@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Children

NamedScan is a business service and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
