How it works

Simple, recurring for 12 months, and tracked automatically.

You earn your tier's share of every payment we collect from each customer you referred, every month of the first 12 months of that customer's subscription. We pay monthly once each payment has cleared.

What your audience gets

  • A free preview on the NamedScan home page.
  • A one time AI visibility report for $5: 15 customer questions and a branded PDF of whether ChatGPT names the business today.
  • Monthly plans, with no ranking guarantees: Starter (Guided) at $199 a month, where you make the changes from a prioritized list; Done For You at $300 a month (recommended), where we make the fixes; or Growth at $500 a month, with up to 3 locations, rescans every 2 weeks, and a monthly strategy call.

Commission applies to the one time report payment and to every monthly plan payment we collect, including renewals, during the first 12 months of each referred customer's plan.

Partner tiers

  • Standard Partner: 30% recurring revenue for the first 12 months of each referred customer's subscription.
  • High Potential Partner: 40% recurring revenue for the first 12 months of each referred customer's subscription, for creators whose audience is a strong fit for NamedScan.
  • Founding / Strategic Partner: up to 50% for the first 12 months, reserved for exceptional creators and offered case by case. It is never offered by default.

Your tier is confirmed by email when you are accepted, together with your partner link.

Simple terms

  • Commission: your tier's share (30% Standard, 40% High Potential, or the rate agreed in writing for a Founding / Strategic Partner) of each payment we actually collect from a customer you referred, during the first 12 months of that customer's subscription. Payments after the first 12 months are not commissionable. Taxes, refunds, and chargebacks are not commissionable, and commission on a refunded payment is deducted from the next payout.
  • Clearing and payouts: a payment counts once it has cleared, which we treat as 30 days after it was collected. We pay cleared commission once a month, by a payment method we agree with you.
  • Tracking: a referral is recorded when a customer checks out within 90 days of visiting through your link. If they later use another partner's link, the most recent link gets the credit. Referrals that cannot be tracked to your link are not eligible.
  • Honest promotion: please disclose that you earn a commission, and never promise rankings or guaranteed recommendations. Nobody controls what ChatGPT says. NamedScan measures visibility and helps owners improve the signals that matter.
  • Not allowed: buying your own plan through your link, bidding on the NamedScan name in search ads, spam, or misleading claims.
  • Changes: we may update these terms with 30 days' notice by email. Commission already earned is always paid.
