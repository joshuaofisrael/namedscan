About NamedScan

NamedScan is an AI visibility service for local businesses. We answer one question: when a customer asks ChatGPT for a business like yours, does it name you?

What we do

We ask ChatGPT, with live web search turned on, the kinds of questions your customers ask, such as "best plumber near me" or "best bakery in my city". We record whether your business is recommended, mentioned, or missing, which competitors are named instead, and which websites ChatGPT cites as sources. You get a dated report with a clear list of fixes.

On monthly plans we also work on the signals ChatGPT can use: your Google Business Profile, consistent listings, clear service and location pages, structured data, a site that search crawlers can read, and recent reviews from real customers. Then we measure again so you can see what changed.

How the score works

The AI visibility score runs from 0 to 100 and combines five parts:

  • Mention rate (40%): how often ChatGPT names your business across the questions we ask.
  • Position (20%): where you appear in the answer when you are named.
  • Share of voice (10%): your share of mentions against the top 5 competitors named.
  • Cited as a source (10%): how often your own website is cited.
  • AI search readiness (20%): how readable your website is for AI search, when it can be reached.

Bands: Low under 25, Emerging 25 to 49, Moderate 50 to 74, Strong 75 and above. The free preview uses the same method with three questions, so it is an estimate.

What we do not do

No one outside OpenAI controls what ChatGPT says, and answers can change from day to day. We work to improve and measure your AI visibility. We do not promise or guarantee rankings or recommendations.
