How AI answer engines cite local businesses

Where ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews pull local business facts from, and how to make your public pages easier to cite.

Updated 2026-11-05. Educational only. NamedScan does not guarantee AI recommendations.

Short answer

Answer engines do not invent a separate Yellow Pages. They remix public web pages, business profiles, reviews and other crawlable sources into a short reply. If your hours, services and neighborhoods are clear in plain text, you are easier to cite than a competitor whose site is mostly images or a PDF brochure.

Common citation sources

  • Your website service and location pages (indexable HTML).
  • Google Business Profile style public listings and major directories.
  • Review sites and local news mentions with concrete facts.
  • Allowing documented search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot when you want ChatGPT search coverage.

What to publish in plain text

State the city and neighborhoods you serve, primary services, hours, phone and a short differentiator. Avoid hiding the only accurate address inside a map widget image. Pair this with Google AI Overviews for local businesses and a monthly prompt check.

Measurement loop

Ask four customer-style prompts for your city and category. Score named, mentioned or absent. Fix the weakest pages, then re-check. NamedScan’s AI visibility check is built for that loop. We do not guarantee recommendations.

Frequently asked questions

Do AI tools only cite my website?

No. They may cite directories, reviews, news and other public pages. Your site still needs clear facts so those sources agree.

Should I block AI crawlers?

Only if you intentionally want to stay out of those products. Blocking search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot can keep you out of ChatGPT search answers.

Is a PDF brochure enough?

Usually not. Prefer HTML service pages with the same facts in readable text.

How often should I re-check citations?

Monthly is a practical cadence for local businesses, or after a major site or profile change.
