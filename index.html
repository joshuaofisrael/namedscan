What the report looks like

Every report is built from real answers collected at scan time. AI answers change often, so each report is dated and repeatable.

  • Visibility score (0 to 100) based on how often, and how prominently, you are recommended.
  • Question by question results from ChatGPT with live web search.
  • The exact questions tested and a summary of each answer.
  • Who gets named instead, so you can see what they do differently.
  • Fix list: listings, reviews, website content, and structured data, in priority order.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is an AI visibility scan?

We ask ChatGPT, with live web search, the kinds of questions your customers ask, like "best dentist near me" or "who installs solar in my town", and record whether your business is recommended, mentioned, or missing. The result is a dated PDF report.

Can you guarantee I will show up in ChatGPT?

No, and nobody honestly can. No one controls what ChatGPT says. What we do is work to improve the signals it relies on (listings, reviews, website content, and structured data) and measure the results on the schedule for your plan so you can see what is changing.

Which AI assistant do you check?

ChatGPT with live web search. That is the only assistant we check.

Is the free preview really free?

The full AI visibility report, with every question, competitor, source, and fix, plus a PDF, is $5 one time. Monthly plans are Starter (Guided) at $199, Done For You at $300, or Growth at $500. How is this different from regular SEO?

SEO focuses on ranking in search results. AI visibility is about whether ChatGPT names your business in its answers. The two overlap, since ChatGPT often draws on the same listings, reviews, and websites, which is why the fix list covers both.
