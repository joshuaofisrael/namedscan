What the report looks like

Every report is built from real answers collected at scan time. AI answers change often, so each report is dated and repeatable.

  • Visibility score (0 to 100) based on how often, and how prominently, you are recommended.
  • Engine by engine results for ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.
  • The exact questions tested and a summary of each answer.
  • Who gets named instead, so you can see what they do differently.
  • Fix list: listings, reviews, website content, and structured data, in priority order.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is an AI visibility scan?

We ask AI assistants the kinds of questions your customers ask, like "best dentist near me" or "who installs solar in my town", and record whether your business is recommended, mentioned, or missing. The result is a dated PDF report.

Can you guarantee I will show up in ChatGPT?

No, and nobody honestly can. No one controls what AI assistants say. What we do is work to improve the signals these tools rely on (listings, reviews, website content, structured data) and measure the results every month so you can see what is changing.

Which AI assistants do you check?

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini today. We may add more as they become common ways customers search.

Is the free scan really free?

Yes. One full scan and PDF report, no card needed. If you want ongoing scans or help with fixes, the paid plans are optional.

How is this different from regular SEO?

SEO focuses on ranking in search results. AI visibility is about whether assistants name your business in their answers. The two overlap, since AI tools often draw on the same listings, reviews and websites, which is why the fix list covers both.
