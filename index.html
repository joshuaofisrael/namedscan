How local businesses show up in ChatGPT

More customers now ask ChatGPT which local business to hire, and the answer usually names only a few. ChatGPT recommendations for businesses depend on public information it can find and understand. There is no switch to flip. These are the signals we review in every AI visibility report and work on in Done For You.

Google Business Profile

Accurate categories, services, hours, and photos, with replies to customer reviews. It is one of the places search tools check first for a local business.

Consistent listings

The same business name, address, and phone number on your website, Google, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, and other directories.

Clear service pages

A page for each main service and city that says who you help and how to reach you.

Structured data

LocalBusiness and FAQ schema markup, so search engines and AI assistants can read the facts on your site without guessing.

Crawl access

A site setup and robots.txt file that do not block the search crawlers ChatGPT search relies on, including OpenAI's own search crawler.

Genuine reviews

Recent reviews from real customers on the sites AI answers cite. The fix list shows where you are thin. We never write or buy reviews.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is an AI visibility scan?

We ask ChatGPT, with live web search, the kinds of questions your customers ask, like "best plumber near me" or "best bakery in my city", and record whether your business is recommended, mentioned, or missing. The result is a dated PDF report.

How does ChatGPT decide which local businesses to recommend?

When someone asks ChatGPT for a local business with web search on, it searches the web, reads what it finds, and writes a short answer that names a few businesses. OpenAI says ChatGPT search works with third party search providers and can use a person's approximate location for local results. It can only name businesses it can find and understand, so clear and consistent information across your website, your Google Business Profile, other listings, and review sites matters. No one outside OpenAI controls the answer, and answers can change from day to day.

How do I show up in ChatGPT?

There is no switch to flip. ChatGPT can only recommend a business it can find and understand. The practical work is a complete Google Business Profile, the same name, address, and phone number across your listings, clear service and location pages, structured data, a site that search crawlers can read, and recent reviews from real customers. NamedScan measures whether you are named today and, on monthly plans, works on those signals. We do not promise or guarantee rankings or recommendations.

Can you guarantee I will show up in ChatGPT?

No, and nobody honestly can. No one controls what ChatGPT says. What we do is work to improve the signals it relies on (listings, reviews, website content, and structured data) and measure the results on the schedule for your plan so you can see what is changing.

Which AI assistant do you check?

ChatGPT with live web search. That is the only assistant we check.

SEO focuses on ranking in search results. AI visibility is about whether ChatGPT names your business in its answers. The two overlap, since ChatGPT often draws on the same listings, reviews, and websites, which is why the fix list covers both.
