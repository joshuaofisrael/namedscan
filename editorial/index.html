Editorial policy

This is the editorial and sourcing policy for NamedScan.

NamedScan reports what a ChatGPT session with live web search actually returned for specific customer-style questions on a given date. We do not invent mentions, citations, or scores. The method and the 0 to 100 score are explained on the about page.

Answers from ChatGPT can change from day to day. When we publish a figure, we date it. If we later find an error in our own notes or on a page, we correct the page.

We do not promise or guarantee rankings or recommendations. For privacy and legal terms, see the privacy and terms pages. For images, see the image policy.
