What this check does

This is the same free preview as the form on the NamedScan home page. It is an AI visibility checker for one question: when a customer asks ChatGPT for a business like yours, does it name you?

The check sends three customer style questions to ChatGPT with live web search. They are the kind of question a local customer asks, such as who to hire in your city. The questions do not include your business name. ChatGPT searches the web and writes a short answer. We record whether your business is named, and which business is named instead.

The estimated score runs from 0 to 100 and uses the same method as the full report:

  • Mention rate (40%): how often ChatGPT names your business across the questions we ask.
  • Position (20%): where you appear in the answer when you are named.
  • Share of voice (10%): your share of mentions against the top 5 competitors named.
  • Cited as a source (10%): how often your own website is cited.
  • AI search readiness (20%): how readable your website is for AI search, when it can be reached.

Because this check uses three questions, the score is an estimate. The full report asks 15. Bands: Low under 25, Emerging 25 to 49, Moderate 50 to 74, Strong 75 and above.

How to read the result

The result is a dated snapshot of those three answers. It is not a rank that ChatGPT publishes, and it is not a promise about the next answer.

  • Estimated score and band. Low is under 25, Emerging is 25 to 49, Moderate is 50 to 74, and Strong is 75 and above. A higher band means you were named more often, or more prominently, in this snapshot.
  • Named in X of 3. That is how many of the three answers included your business.
  • Named instead. The business listed here is the one ChatGPT named most often in this check, when another business stood out.
